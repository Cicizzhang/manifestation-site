01 / The Question

What must mind, reality, and personal identity be like for manifestation to be metaphysically possible?

This project is an evolving philosophical inquiry. The framework presented here is not a finished theory, but a record of questions, arguments, revisions, and experiments as the research develops.

02 / Metaphysical Conditions

Three problems sit at the center of the inquiry.

I

Mind

Can assumption do more than represent reality?

What kind of mind would be required for imagination, perception, or assumption to participate in what becomes real?

II

Reality

How independent of us is the reality we experience?

Manifestation appears to require a relationship between inner assumption and outer experience. What conception of reality could make such a relationship possible?

III

Self

Who is the “I” that assumes?

If identity can change through assumption, what constitutes personal identity before, during, and after that transformation?
